# `chat-v6-scratch` under the shipped decoder

**Not part of the Phase-1..5 research protocol.** No number here is a reported
figure. This is a chat-side diagnostic: it trains nothing, changes no default,
and does **not** touch `experiments/chat/SHIPPED`. Whether to repoint that file
is the owner's call, and this note is the evidence he would be ruling on.

Every count below is regenerated by
`python scripts/chat/v6_scratch_summary.py` from the stored pools and written to
`experiments/chat/_quality/v6scratch_summary.json`. Rates are `k/n` with a Wilson
95 % interval, per `CONVENTIONS.md` §1.

## 1. What was fixed before any draw, and by whom

`chat-v6-scratch` is the fresh-init 84,000-step run on `chat-v3d-aligned`'s exact
recipe. `QUALITY_v6.md` §2 set it aside on the old `n = 8` headline (0.2679
against 0.2969) and its held-out bpc is worse on every source (weighted 1.2399
against 1.1743, same mixture). It carried one unread lead: the highest
`pool_by_kind.topic` of every committed battery file (0.1191; the next is
0.0957). It had **never been
scored with the `n = 256` echo decoder**, which is the decoder the REPL ships.

On 2026-09-20 a design panel asked for that reading and fixed how it would be
read, before any draw existed:

- command: `echo_holdout.py --ckpt chat-v6-scratch/ckpt_best.pt --n 256 --lam 0.6
  --seeds 6 --set {wide, heldout, fresh}`;
- reading: the **WIDE oracle**, "different from the incumbent" **only outside
  52-106 of 360** (the incumbent's four training seeds read 67-91). Inside the
  band is `unresolved`;
- role: "a plain diagnostic for choosing a parent, NOT a go/no-go gate".

## 2. The plumbing check that came first

The draws were made on 2026-09-21 from a clean worktree at `4446951`'s decode
path, with an absolute `--ckpt`. Before anything else the incumbent's HELDOUT
pool was redrawn the same way: **all 120 rows identical** to the committed
`v12_heldout_chat-v3d-aligned.json`. So a worktree with absolute paths is a
verified way to produce chat evidence, and what follows is the same instrument
the committed incumbent files were made with.

**Pre-stated verdict: the WIDE oracle is 128 of 360, outside 52-106 —
different from the incumbent, upward.**

The oracle is a property of the *pool* — whether any of 256 drafts names the
noun — so this is not the echo tier flattering itself (`QUALITY_v8.md` §7): the
checkpoint **drafts** the requested noun about half again as often. On FRESH,
the hard nouns no corpus slot ever drilled, it drafts them no more often than
the incumbent does. It is better at the nouns the corpus asks about, not at
nouns in general.

## 4. Guards — written after §3 was seen and before each was run

This ordering is a limitation and is stated as one: the guards are post hoc to
the topic result. They were written down (with the rule below) before any of
them was computed. A guard reads **worse** only if `chat-v6-scratch`'s Wilson
interval lies wholly on the bad side of the incumbent's pooled four-seed rate
(`snnchat.quality.resolves_against`). None is a ship gate.

| guard | `chat-v6-scratch` | incumbent | reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `story_dodge`, echo on, λ = 0.6 (`lambda_dodge.py --n 256 --seeds 6`) | 0.1111 (8/72), [0.057, 0.204] | 7 / 8 / 6 / 7 of 72; pooled 0.0972 (28/288) | `unresolved` — not resolved worse |
| memory probe, distance 0 (`memory_probe.py` defaults) | told 1/80, untold 1/80 | told 0/80, untold 0/80 (committed) | at the floor, like the incumbent |
| social / identity, committed battery, `n = 1, λ = 0` picks | 20/20, 16/16 | 20/20 and 15-16 of 16 | no difference |
| own-model logP/char of the selected reply | WIDE −0.3611, HELDOUT −0.3396, FRESH −0.3527 | per-seed ranges −0.3634..−0.3508, −0.3452..−0.3362, −0.3555..−0.3449 | inside the range on all three |
| pool UER@200 (instrument from `chat/v14-subject-tier` at `8bbbd44`; qualifying fraction ≥ 0.997) | WIDE 0.2184, HELDOUT 0.2275, FRESH 0.2134 | per-seed 0.2147..0.2482, 0.2142..0.2501, 0.2053..0.2355 | inside the range on all three |

Three things those rows do not say:

- **The logP row cannot rank fluency across models.** Each model is scored under
  itself. On the `story_dodge` prompts `chat-v6-scratch`'s selected replies read
  −0.6949 against the incumbent seeds' −0.6139..−0.6718, outside the range on the
  bad side. A worse-bpc model scoring its own text lower is expected; it is
  neither evidence the replies read worse nor evidence they do not.
- **The selected-reply UER is at the top of the incumbent's range** (WIDE
  92/359 against a per-seed maximum of 91/360; HELDOUT 40/120 against 38/120).
  That is the expected direction — on-topic drafts carry a higher UER than
  off-topic ones, and it selects more of them — but it was read without
  `uer_decomposition`, which `CONVENTIONS.md` §7 (on the v14 branch) requires
  before a UER difference between two arms is quoted. Read it as "not flagged",
  not as "no difference".
- **UER and the side-by-side agree on the thing that matters to a reader:** the
  stories drift exactly as much. `V6_SCRATCH_SIDE_BY_SIDE.md` (prompts chosen by
  a rule fixed before any reply was read) has *"there was a penguin. The pig
  loved to play…"*. It names the noun more often. It does not stay on it.

## 5. What this licenses, and what it does not

- **It supports a claim about this checkpoint against the shipped checkpoint**,
  under the shipped decoder: WIDE selected 125/360 [0.300, 0.398] against the
  shipped file's own 69/360 [0.154, 0.236]. That gap is resolved by sampler
  noise alone, which is the only noise a comparison between two frozen files has.
- **It supports no claim about the from-scratch recipe.** `n = 1` training seed;
  its SD_seed is unmeasured; the incumbent's SD on the WIDE selected count is
  about 11 of 360 (`CONVENTIONS.md` §3, third bullet).
- **The August discard was decided by the wrong columns.** The headline it lost
  is dominated by 20 `list` draws (`QUALITY_v8.md`), and bpc does not track what
  a reader sees — a 0.066 bpc deficit coexists here with the largest topicality
  gap this package has measured between two checkpoints.
- It remains the worse language model by bpc, it remembers a told fact no better
  (1/80), and nothing about list- or fact-following moved (committed battery:
  `list` 1/20, `fact` 2/28).
